A two-line warning to the past self: line one stops a 2 a.m. email to the
boss, line two traces the cause and effect — waiting, coffee instead, and
the promotion going to the one who apologised, not the one who was right.

## The entry

**Past me, don't hit send on that 2 a.m. 'per my last email' to your boss — sleep on it.
You waited, brought him coffee instead, and the promotion went to the one who apologised, not the one who was right.**

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). No network, no randomness, no
arguments: the entry is fixed, so every run produces the same bytes.
